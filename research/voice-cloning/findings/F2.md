# F2: OSS cloning landscape

## Findings

### [1] Coqui XTTS-v2 is zero-shot cross-lingual TTS cloning from a ~3s clip, 16 languages including Spanish and English, fine-tunable, 24 kHz, streaming <200ms
- quote: "ⓍTTS is a super cool Text-to-Speech model that lets you clone voices in different languages by using just a quick 3-second audio clip... As of now, XTTS-v2 supports 16 languages: English (en), Spanish (es), French (fr), German (de), Italian (it), Portuguese (pt), Polish (pl), Turkish (tr), Russian (ru), Dutch (nl), Czech (cs), Arabic (ar), Chinese (zh-cn), Japanese (ja), Hungarian (hu) and Korean (ko)."
- url: https://tts.readthedocs.io/en/latest/models/xtts.html
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (TTS 0.22.0 docs)
- confidence: high

## Dead ends
- Official documented VRAM floors are sparse: only GPT-SoVITS publishes explicit finetune VRAM (8–14GB via Changelog). XTTS/F5-TTS/CosyVoice/MaskGCT docs give no canonical "min VRAM" number — community figures exist but were not fetched from a citable primary page, so omitted rather than guessed.
- Coqui is defunct; XTTS-v2 docs are frozen at TTS 0.22.0; no active upstream for new languages or license relicense. TTSC/CPML does not permit commercial deployment without a (now unobtainable) Coqui commercial grant.
- MaskGCT has no official Docker image in Amphion (no Dockerfile surfaced); runtime is conda + Jupyter/Gradio/inference script only.
- RVC is not a TTS cloner — it converts existing speech to a target timbre; cannot synthesize from text alone.
- Fish Speech commercial license terms beyond "contact business@fish.audio" are not public.
- F5-Spanish license metadata is internally inconsistent (cc-by-nc-4.0 card vs CC0-1.0 body) — treat as non-commercial until clarified.
