# F5: Alternatives and quality tradeoffs

## Findings

### [1] Zero-shot voice conversion (Seed-VC) can stand in for full cloning with only 1–30s of reference audio and no training
- quote: "Without any training, it is able to clone a voice given a reference speech of 1~30 seconds."
- url: https://github.com/Plachtaa/seed-vc
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-11 (arXiv 2411.09943); repo archived 2025-11-21
- confidence: high

### [2] Seed-VC few-shot fine-tune needs as little as 1 utterance / 100 steps (~2 min on T4) to boost speaker similarity
- quote: "We support further fine-tuning on custom data to increase performance on specific speaker/speakers, with extremely low data requirement (minimum 1 utterance per speaker) and extremely fast training speed (minimum 100 steps, 2 min on T4)!"
- url: https://github.com/Plachtaa/seed-vc
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-11
- confidence: high

### [3] Seed-VC zero-shot beats OpenVoice/CosyVoice on speaker similarity and intelligibility (SECS 0.868, WER 11.99)
- quote: "| Seed-VC(Ours) | **0.8676** | **11.99** | **2.92** | 3.42 | 3.97 | 3.11 |" (vs OpenVoice SECS 0.7547 WER 15.46; CosyVoice SECS 0.8440 WER 18.98; GT WER 8.02)
- url: https://github.com/Plachtaa/seed-vc/blob/main/EVAL.md
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-11
- confidence: high

### [4] Zero-shot Seed-VC with a 1-utterance prompt often matches or beats per-speaker RVCv2 models on SECS/CER, but slightly loses DNSMOS
- quote: "Despite Seed-VC is not trained on the target speakers, and only one random utterance is used as prompt, it still constantly outperforms speaker-specific RVCv2 models in terms of speaker similarity (SECS) and intelligibility (CER) ... However, it is observed that Seed-VC's audio quality (DNSMOS) is slightly lower than RVCv2."
- url: https://github.com/Plachtaa/seed-vc/blob/main/EVAL.md
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-11
- confidence: high

### [5] Practitioner RVC training-data floor: ~10 minutes target; 40s is not enough; 3–4h is common upper end
- quote: "Your training data is good but you need more of it, 40 seconds is not enough to train a good RVC model. Aim for 10 minutes if possible but the more the merrier, I've done models with upwards of 4 hours worth of training data so there really is no such thing as 'too much data'."
- url: https://github.com/RVC-Project/Retrieval-based-Voice-Conversion-WebUI/issues/2025
- source_type: community
- published: 2024-04-30
- confidence: medium

### [6] RVC quality saturates quickly on data/epochs: 38 min of audio gains most by ~200 epochs (not 1000–2500)
- quote: "with 38 minutes of audio you get the most by 200 epochs. Training for 2000 is insanity. Even the pretrain model with 44 hours of audio was trained only to 636 epochs."
- url: https://github.com/RVC-Project/Retrieval-based-Voice-Conversion-WebUI/issues/2332
- source_type: community
- published: 2024-10-18
- confidence: medium

### [7] GPT-SoVITS is the strongest few-shot TTS alternative: 5s zero-shot, ~1 min for fine-tune; RTF 0.014–0.028 on modern GPU
- quote: "Zero-shot TTS: Input a 5-second vocal sample and experience instant text-to-speech conversion. Few-shot TTS: Fine-tune the model with just 1 minute of training data for improved voice similarity and realism. ... RTF(inference speed) of GPT-SoVITS v2 ProPlus: 0.028 tested in 4060Ti, 0.014 tested in 4090 (1400words~=4min, inference time is 3.36s), 0.526 in M4 CPU."
- url: https://github.com/RVC-Boss/GPT-SoVITS
- source_type: primary
- published: unknown (active 2025–2026)
- confidence: high

### [8] StyleTTS2 multi-speaker + 1h finetune (~1k samples) is viable but slower to train and slightly worse than 24h from-scratch
- quote: "The default configuration config_ft.yml finetunes on LJSpeech with 1 hour of speech data (around 1k samples) for 50 epochs. This took about 4 hours to finish on four NVidia A100. The quality is slightly worse (similar to NaturalSpeech on LJSpeech) than LJSpeech model trained from scratch with 24 hours of speech data, which took around 2.5 days to finish on four A100."
- url: https://github.com/yl4579/StyleTTS2
- source_type: primary
- published: 2023-06 (arXiv 2306.07691)
- confidence: high

### [9] Kokoro has no supported fine-tuning path; community reverse-engineering of StyleTTS2 is incomplete and GPU-heavy
- quote: "After about a week of throwing GPU hours and LLM tokens at the problem, I somehow managed to vibe-reverse-engineer a working training pipeline using a patched version of StyleTTS2. ... The bad news: I am way out of my depth on Stage 2 (the prosody predictor). Stage 2 currently degrades the style_encoder and outputs static noise"
- url: https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro/issues/288
- source_type: community
- published: 2026-04-10
- confidence: high

### [10] Kokoro finetuning attempters report the loss/training recipe is the blocker (multi-step GAN), not just missing scripts
- quote: "I've built a small training loop with a very simple loss function that compares a mel spectogram of the reference audio and the generated one. Unfortunately, the loss function you'd need for this model is way more complicated. If it's been trained in a similar way to TTS2, then it requires multi-step training with a GAN."
- url: https://github.com/hexgrad/kokoro/issues/207
- source_type: community
- published: 2025-08-27
- confidence: medium

### [11] Realtime VC (Seed-VC tiny) is practical: ~430ms total latency, 150ms/chunk on RTX 3060 laptop at 10 diffusion steps
- quote: "seed-uvit-xlsr-tiny ... Diffusion Steps 10 ... Latency (ms) 430ms ... Inference Time per Chunk (ms) 150ms"
- url: https://github.com/Plachtaa/seed-vc
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-11
- confidence: high

### [12] VC quality/speed dial: 25 diffusion steps default; 30–50 best quality; 4–10 fastest; CFG off (~0.0) ~1.5× speedup
- quote: "`diffusion-steps` is the number of diffusion steps to use, default is 25, use 30-50 for best quality, use 4-10 for fastest inference ... Inference CFG Rate ... set to 0.0 gains about 1.5x speed-up"
- url: https://github.com/Plachtaa/seed-vc
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-11
- confidence: high

### [13] Hybrid TTS+VC path is architecturally cheap: keep Kokoro/Piper as content source, retarget timbre with zero-shot VC; fine-tune VC only if similarity lags
- quote: "Fine-tuning on custom data allow the model to clone someone's voice more accurately. It will largely improve speaker similarity on particular speakers, but may slightly increase WER."
- url: https://github.com/Plachtaa/seed-vc
- source_type: primary
- published: 2024-11
- confidence: medium

### [14] Realistic sample-duration ladder across alternatives
- quote: "1~30 seconds" (Seed-VC zero-shot); "minimum 1 utterance per speaker" (Seed-VC ft); "5-second vocal sample" / "1 minute of training data" (GPT-SoVITS); "1 hour of speech data (around 1k samples)" (StyleTTS2 ft); "Aim for 10 minutes if possible" (RVC practitioner)
- url: https://github.com/Plachtaa/seed-vc
- source_type: secondary
- published: 2026-09-27
- confidence: high

## Dead ends
- Seed-VC original repo is archived (2025-11-21) — still usable but unmaintained; forks exist (ComfyUI, fast-vc-service, webapi).
- Piper has no first-class voice-conversion path (issue #448 unanswered; `voice_conversion.py` checkpoints unclear).
- Kokoro `voices.pt`-only finetune is a dead end per community reports (whole-model / StyleTTS2 rewrite required).
- No published MOS numbers found for the TTS→RVC/Seed-VC stacked pipeline specifically (DNSMOS/SECS/CER only).

## Suggested follow-ups
- Measure end-to-end RTF of Kokoro/Piper → Seed-VC tiny stacked pipeline vs GPT-SoVITS alone on the target box.
- A/B 30s vs 1min vs 5min reference clips for Seed-VC zero-shot on Spanish + English (SECS/WER not language-matched in EVAL.md).
- Check commercial-optional hybrid (ElevenLabs / OpenAI TTS voice design) latency + Spanish quality for non-local fallback.
- Pilot Seed-VC fine-tune at 100 / 500 / 1000 steps on ~10 min of target speaker to find the similarity/RTF knee.
