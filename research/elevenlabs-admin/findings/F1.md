# F1: ElevenLabs admin and product features

## Findings

### [1] Product surfaces are split into four named platforms: ElevenCreative (no-code web app), ElevenAgents (conversational voice agents), ElevenAPI (REST + SDKs), and Reception AI (AI phone receptionist)
- quote: "ElevenCreative is a no-code web application where creators, producers, and editors generate voiceovers, music, dubs, and studio projects directly in the browser. ElevenAgents is the platform for designing and operating conversational voice agents... ElevenAPI exposes every capability as a REST interface with official Python and TypeScript SDKs... Reception AI is a ready-to-deploy AI phone receptionist for small and medium businesses"
- url: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-27 (fetched)
- confidence: high

### [2] Voices are first-class objects with unique IDs; Voice Library has 10,000+ voices; users can clone from audio or generate from a text description
- quote: "Voices are the speech persona used in audio generation. Each voice has a unique ID — for example, JBFqnCBsd6RMkjVDRZzb — that you select in the dashboard or pass in API requests. ElevenLabs maintains a library of 10,000+ voices. You can also clone a voice from an audio recording or generate one from a text description."
- url: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-27 (fetched)
- confidence: high

### [3] Credits are the single shared consumption unit across every product; TTS is 1 credit/character; credits reset monthly and unused credits roll over up to two months
- quote: "Credits are the unit of consumption shared across every product. Text-to-speech costs one credit per character of input text. Other operations are charged per second of audio processed. Credits reset monthly and unused credits roll over for up to two months."
- url: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-27 (fetched)

### [11] Instant vs Professional Voice Cloning are fundamentally different: IVC is few-shot inference conditioning (immediate, works from <2 min audio, quality ceiling = reference audio); PVC fine-tunes model weights (~30 min high-quality audio, Creator plan+, minutes to train, higher consistency across styles)
- quote: "Instant Voice Cloning works through few-shot adaptation: the model uses your audio sample as a conditioning signal at inference time... Professional Voice Cloning takes a different approach: it fine-tunes the model on your audio samples... ElevenLabs recommends approximately 30 minutes of high-quality audio... It requires plan eligibility. PVC requires a Creator plan or above"
- url: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-api/concepts/voice-cloning.md
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-27 (fetched)
- confidence: high

### [12] Voice cloning includes a voice-verification step (voice captcha) as an ethical/legal safeguard, plus optional speaker separation to isolate a target speaker from multi-speaker source audio before cloning
- quote: "Both IVC and PVC include a voice verification step. This is not a technical requirement of the synthesis — it is an ethical and legal safeguard. The verification process uses voice-captcha technology to confirm that you are the person providing the voice samples... When source audio contains multiple speakers, a speaker separation step can be applied to isolate the target voice before cloning."
- url: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-api/concepts/voice-cloning.md
- source_type: primary
- published: 2026-09-27 (fetched)
- confidence: high
